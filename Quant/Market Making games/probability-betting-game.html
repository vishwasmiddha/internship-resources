Probability Betting

Each round, the house offers bets on dice, cards and coins at posted odds. Some odds are better than fair (a positive-EV edge worth taking); others are traps priced worse than the true probability. Start with 1,000 coins, find the edges, size your stakes, and grow your bankroll before the clock runs out.

Read the odds

Posted odds are X:1. Compare them to the fair odds (1−p)/p implied by each event's real probability.

Take the edge

Bet when offered odds beat fair odds. Pass — or fade with a Put bet — when they don't.

Size with Kelly

Your post-game report grades every stake against the Kelly-optimal bet for the edge you had.
